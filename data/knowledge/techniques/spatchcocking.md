# Spatchcocking
<!-- Source: https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Cookbook:Spatchcocking (CC BY-SA 4.0) -->

Spatchcocking is a technique where a whole bird is split almost in two, leaving the sternum but removing the spine. It is a type of butterflying, which flattens the entire bird out. This type of preparation is often used for grilling.
