# Cheese Substitutions

### Substituting Pecorino Romano
- **Best match:** Parmigiano-Reggiano or Grana Padano at a 1:1 ratio.
- **Flavor note:** Pecorino is made from sheep's milk and is saltier and sharper than Parmigiano. If swapping in Parmesan, add an extra pinch of salt to balance.
- **Alternative:** Spanish Manchego (aged 6+ months), finely grated.

### Substituting Parmigiano-Reggiano (Parmesan)
- **Best match:** Grana Padano (1:1 ratio, slightly milder, melts identically) or Pecorino Romano (reduce dish salt slightly).
- **Dairy-free match:** Nutritional yeast (provides savory umami; use half the quantity and adjust to taste).

### Substituting Mozzarella
- **For melting on pizza or bakes:** Provolone, Fontina, Monterey Jack, or mild young Gouda (all melt smoothly at similar temperatures).
- **Fresh mozzarella substitute:** Fior di latte or mild Queso Oaxaca.

### Substituting Ricotta Cheese
- **Best match:** Whole-milk cottage cheese pulsed in a blender or food processor until smooth (1:1 ratio).
- **Alternative:** Mascarpone (richer and creamier; best in fillings) or well-drained firm silken tofu mashed with a pinch of salt and lemon juice.
