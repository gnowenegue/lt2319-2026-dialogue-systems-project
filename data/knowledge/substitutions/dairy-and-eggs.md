# Dairy and Egg Substitutions

### Substituting Heavy Cream
- **Best match for cooking/sauces:** 3/4 cup whole milk + 1/3 cup melted unsalted butter per cup of heavy cream. Simmer gently; do not boil aggressively.
- **For soups and curries:** Full-fat coconut milk or canned evaporated milk at a 1:1 ratio. Coconut milk adds a subtle sweetness.
- **For thickening without butter:** Greek yogurt or sour cream whisked in at the very end off heat to prevent curdling.

### Substituting Buttermilk
- **Best match:** 1 cup milk (whole or 2%) + 1 tablespoon lemon juice or white vinegar. Let sit for 5 minutes until slightly curdled.
- **Alternative:** Plain yogurt or sour cream thinned with milk at a 3:4 ratio (3 parts yogurt to 1 part milk).

### Substituting Whole Milk
- **Best match:** 1/2 cup evaporated milk + 1/2 cup water, or 7/8 cup skim milk + 2 tablespoons melted butter.
- **Dairy-free match:** Unsweetened oat milk or soy milk (neutral flavor and similar protein/fat ratio).

### Substituting Sour Cream
- **Best match:** Plain whole-milk Greek yogurt at a 1:1 ratio. Same acidity, slightly thicker texture, lower fat.
- **Alternative:** Crème fraîche (richer, less tangy) or blended cottage cheese with a squeeze of lemon juice.

### Substituting Eggs for Binding and Moisture
- **For binding in meatballs or patties:** 1/4 cup mashed potato, 1/4 cup unsweetened applesauce, or 2 tablespoons tomato paste per egg.
- **For baking/coatings (Flax Egg):** 1 tablespoon ground flaxseed + 3 tablespoons warm water; rest 5 minutes until gelatinous (replaces 1 whole egg).
- **For breading station:** Milk, buttermilk, or Dijon mustard mixed with a splash of water to adhere breadcrumbs without raw egg.
