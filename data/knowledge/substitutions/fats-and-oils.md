# Cooking Fats and Oils Substitutions

### Substituting Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO)
- **For dressings and finishing:** Regular olive oil, avocado oil, or walnut oil.
- **For sautéing/pan-frying:** Pure olive oil, light olive oil, or avocado oil (higher smoke points, won't scorch or turn bitter).
- **Culinary caveat:** Do not use EVOO for high-heat searing or deep frying; it has a low smoke point (~190°C / 375°F) and will burn.

### Substituting Butter in Cooking
- **For sautéing and pan-cooking:** Olive oil, ghee (clarified butter), or neutral vegetable oil at a 3:4 ratio (use 3/4 tablespoon oil per 1 tablespoon butter).
- **For richness/finishing sauces:** Ghee or heavy cream (finish off heat).

### Substituting Neutral Cooking Oils
- **Interchangeable options:** Canola oil, vegetable oil, sunflower oil, corn oil, and grapeseed oil can all substitute for each other at a 1:1 ratio.
- **High-heat searing alternative:** Peanut oil or avocado oil (smoke points above 230°C / 450°F).

### Substituting Toasted Sesame Oil
- **Best match:** Neutral oil (like canola or peanut) mixed with a few drops of tahini (sesame paste) or lightly toasted white sesame seeds.
- **Culinary caveat:** Toasted sesame oil is a flavoring oil, not a cooking fat. Add it at the end of cooking off heat, never as the primary frying oil.
