# Thickeners and Pantry Staple Substitutions

### Cornstarch vs. All-Purpose Flour
- **Conversion:** 1 tablespoon of cornstarch has the thickening power of roughly 2 tablespoons of all-purpose flour (2:1 ratio).
- **Application (Slurry):** Always dissolve cornstarch in cold water first (equal parts water and cornstarch) before stirring into a simmering sauce. Never dump dry cornstarch into hot liquid or it will clump instantly.
- **Visual difference:** Cornstarch yields a glossy, translucent sauce (common in stir-fries); flour yields an opaque, velvety sauce (gravies and bisques).

### Substituting Breadcrumbs
- **Best match:** Panko breadcrumbs (lighter and crispier; 1:1 substitute).
- **Alternative:** Crushed saltine or Ritz crackers, crushed cornflakes (unsweetened), or rolled oats ground briefly in a blender.

### Substituting Tomato Paste
- **Best match:** Tomato purée or tomato sauce simmered down until thick. Use 2 to 3 tablespoons of tomato sauce for every 1 tablespoon of tomato paste, reducing other liquids slightly.
- **Alternative:** Ketchup (only in barbecue sauces or meatloaf; reduce sugar elsewhere in the recipe).

### Substituting Lemon Juice for Acidity
- **Best match:** White wine vinegar, apple cider vinegar, or rice vinegar at a 1:2 ratio (use half as much vinegar as lemon juice, as vinegar is sharper).
- **Alternative:** Lime juice at a 1:1 ratio.
